# Community code of conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, technically constructive, and
welcoming across experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment, threats,
targeted insults, hateful content, unsolicited disclosure of personal
information, and intimidation are not tolerated.

Review ideas and code on their merits. Criticize implementations, not people.
Maintain privacy when sharing sample images, diagnostics and video clips; only
share media you have the right to distribute.

Report misconduct privately to repository maintainers using a private channel
when available. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation,
or ban repeat or serious violators after considering context and impact.
This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and
project-affiliated community spaces.
